Walktober

A little nature hunt that turns your photos into collectible stickers and matches them against quests using AI running entirely on your device. Photos never leave the browser, and it works offline once cached.

U²-Net-P handles sticker cutouts; CLIP handles quest matching. I precomputed text embeddings at build time so the browser only needs the vision model.

  • Nuxt
  • ONNX
  • On-device AI
  • Offline-first
